# Use Fleet day to day

Most days you do three things in Fleet: answer what waits on you, file what you noticed, and glance at what the fleet is doing. This path takes one small change from request to merge, then shows each of those steps. When you are done you will know where to look for anything that needs you.

## The big picture

- **Cockpit.** Everything that waits on a person, from every source, in one list: yours under **Mine**, the rest under **Unassigned** and **Team**. You answer it, or snooze it until a date.
- **Filed by me.** Every feature request, bug and task you filed, on its own tab in the Cockpit beside **All items**. You write it in plain words. Fleet works out its priority and where it belongs.
- **Queue.** What the fleet will take next, what it is holding back, and what a machine is running right now.

The three make one loop. You file from the buttons at the foot of the sidebar: **Feature request**, **Report a bug** and **File a task**. The fleet takes it from there. When it needs you, to answer an interview, accept a proposal or confirm a bug, it appears in the Cockpit and its count shows in the sidebar. Your answer goes back to the session that asked, and the work carries on.

Most of it is questions. Some are jobs only a person can do, such as creating a credential: you do it, then hand it back. A customer's report waits for a person to read it before any agent does. The Queue is where you look when you want to know what the fleet is doing. You never have to watch it for the work to move.

## Read these in order

1. **Take one feature request to a merged change** (start/your-first-change): File a small feature request, answer Fleet's questions, accept its proposal, and watch it become a merged pull request.
2. **Find your way around the app** (start/find-your-way): A tour of the sidebar: the daily pages at the top, then Fleet, Docs, Settings and System.
3. **Work through the Cockpit** (guides/daily/cockpit): One list, opening on what waits on you: answer each item on its own page, narrow it by state, customer or change date, save a view, or snooze an item.
4. **Answer a question** (guides/daily/answer-a-question): Answer what an agent asked you, approve a design it drew, or send the question back with instructions.
5. **File a feature request, a bug or a task** (guides/daily/file-work): Hand the fleet something new in plain words: a feature request to place and shape, a bug to reproduce, or a task to build.

## When you need it

- **After you file** (guides/daily/after-you-file): Say where a feature request goes, answer the interview, accept or send back what it becomes, and confirm the bug the fleet reproduced.
- **Take work by hand** (guides/daily/take-it-by-hand): Do a job only a person can do and hand it back, or take an issue off the fleet and drive it yourself.
- **Read the Queue** (guides/daily/the-queue): See what the fleet will work on next, what it is holding back and what is running, and change an issue's priority, labels or comments.
- **Find anything** (guides/daily/find-anything): Find a filing, an issue, a planning document or a page by its key or its words, and jump anywhere with the palette.
- **Screen what customers send** (guides/daily/customer-reports): Read a customer's bug report or feature request before any agent does, then approve it, take it up, or turn it away.
- **Review a pull request Fleet opened** (guides/daily/review-a-pull-request): Hold a Fleet pull request's merge with a review comment or a request for changes, and know how Fleet answers it.
