# Narrow Fleet to your programs

When you are done every program will name the people responsible for it, and you will be able to narrow the Programs list to the ones that are yours with one control.

## Name who is responsible

Each program can name one or more people who are responsible for it. Anyone on your team can set it.

1. [you] **Open Programs.** The **Responsible** column shows who is responsible for each program, or a button to assign someone.
2. [you] **Click it on the program's row.** A box asks **Who is responsible for** that program, and lists the people who have signed in to your Fleet. **Search by name or email** narrows it.
3. [you] **Pick the people and press Save.** Tick one or more people, then press **Save**; **Cancel** leaves it as it was. Each person you picked now finds the program under **Assigned to me**.

> **Why:** This changes what each person sees, never what they may open. A program you are not responsible for is still one click away from anyone.

## Narrow the Programs list

The Programs list opens on every program. Under **Filter**, choose **Assigned** and then **Assigned to me** to see only the programs you are responsible for. It shows as a chip while it narrows; its × shows every program again. **Unassigned** shows the programs nobody is responsible for yet.

If you are responsible for nothing yet, the list says so. Ask a teammate to set it, or clear the filter.

## What the Cockpit shows you

The [Cockpit](guides/daily/cockpit) narrows by person, not by program. It opens on what waits on you: the items assigned to you, and the questions asked of you by name. Its **Assignee** filter shows **Me** while it narrows; remove the chip to see everyone's, or pick **Nobody** for what no one is assigned yet. The sidebar count counts what waits on you.

> **Careful:** **Assigned to me** appears only when Fleet knows who you are. On a Fleet with no sign-in, such as one run on your own machine, every program shows, and the Cockpit opens on everyone's items.
